# Training Guide — Reviewers

Reviewers inspect submitted content, ask for changes, approve, or reject.

## The review inbox

1. Log in at `/admin/login`.
2. Open **Review Inbox** from the dashboard.
3. The inbox lists items with status **In Review**.

## Reviewing a submission

1. Open the item to see the full content, attached documents, and history.
2. Read the body, check the title, and verify the publish/close dates.
3. Click **Approve** or **Reject**.

## Rejecting with a comment

1. Click **Reject**.
2. Enter a clear comment explaining what the editor must fix.
3. The editor receives an email with your comment.
4. The editor can update the item and resubmit.

## Publishing approved content

1. After approving, click **Publish**.
2. The item becomes visible on the public site.
3. The original editor receives a confirmation email.

## Best practices

- Check facts and spelling before approving.
- Ensure PDFs are readable and malware-scan clean.
- Do not publish content with placeholder text.
